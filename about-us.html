About Sweet Crumbs Bakery

Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a small bakery business focused on fresh baked products and personalised customer service.

Our aim is to provide customers with enjoyable baked goods that are prepared carefully and presented beautifully. The business serves customers looking for everyday treats as well as cakes and orders for special occasions.

  • Freshly prepared baked products
  • Friendly customer service
  • Custom options for celebrations
  • Simple and reliable ordering
